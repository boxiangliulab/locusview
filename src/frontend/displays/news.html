Contributing data

Data contributions are planned for a later phase (see ADR-0007). The intended process:

1
Format your summary statistics
A TSV with columns: variant_id chr pos pvalue beta se, plus an LD reference panel (VCF or PLINK).
2
Contact us
Email metadata and a sample file to data@locusview.org, including cohort description, phenotype, ancestry, and access restrictions.
3
Processing & integration
Human review, then a standardized mapping pipeline before the dataset becomes queryable.
